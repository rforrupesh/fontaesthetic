No — they're standard Unicode characters styled to look like different fonts. Nothing needs to be installed, and the text renders correctly on any device or platform that supports Unicode.

Will this text work everywhere?

Most styles display correctly across major social platforms and apps. A few rare symbols may not render on very old devices or systems with limited font support.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Generate and copy as many styles as you like — there's no sign-up or limit.

Can search engines index this text?
