Have you ever noticed how clean and easy-to-read the text looks when you use ChatGPT? Its simple letters, balanced spacing, and smooth appearance make conversations comfortable to read. You might want to use the same font for your own website, documents, social media posts, or designs.

ChatGPT's font appearance can depend on your device and operating system. OpenAI also has its own custom typeface called **OpenAI Sans**, which is part of its brand identity. Let's explore the ChatGPT font, its appearance, similar fonts, and ways to create stylish text.

## What Font Does ChatGPT Use?

OpenAI Sans is the custom typeface used in OpenAI's brand identity. According to the [official OpenAI Design Guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/), it combines geometric shapes with smooth curves and a friendly appearance.

However, the font displayed in the ChatGPT interface can vary across devices. OpenAI's [UI guidelines](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/concepts/ui-guidelines) explain that platform-native system fonts are used for readability, including SF Pro on iOS and a sans-serif font on Android.

OpenAI Sans comes in five main weights: Light, Regular, Medium, Semibold, and Bold. Each weight helps create a different text appearance while keeping the same overall design.

## What Does the ChatGPT Font Look Like?

OpenAI Sans has a modern and minimal appearance. Its letters have smooth curves, balanced proportions, and a clean structure. This makes it suitable for websites, headings, and everyday reading.

The font does not depend on decorative shapes or unusual letter designs. Instead, it keeps the text simple and gives more attention to readability.

You can explore the typeface and its design details on the [OpenAI Brand Guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/). The page also explains how OpenAI uses typography as part of its visual identity.
