---
title: "What Is Unicode Fancy Text, Really?"
description: "How bold, italic and gothic-looking text on social media is actually made of real Unicode characters, not fonts."
pubDate: 2026-08-01
---

Every "fancy font" you see on Instagram bios isn't a font at all — it's a different set of Unicode characters that happen to look styled. That's why it copies and pastes perfectly anywhere.
