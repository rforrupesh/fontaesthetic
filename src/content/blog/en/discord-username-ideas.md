## Discord Username and Display Name Ideas

There are two primary types of names on Discord: user names and display names. Although they may seem quite similar, their functions are different.

**Your username** is your unique account identifier which can be used by others to search for or add you to Discord. It has to consist of at least 2 and no more than 32 characters. Only lowercase Latin letters, digits, underscores and periods are allowed for a user name.

**Your display name** is the name that other users usually see in chats or in your profile information. Capital letters, whitespaces, emojis, special symbols, and stylish Unicode characters can be included in it.

| **Feature** | **Username** | **Display name** |
|---|---|---|
| Main purpose | Identifies your account | Shows how your name appears to others |
| Must be unique | **Yes** | **No** |
| Stylish Unicode characters | Not allowed | Can be used |
| Where people see it | Search, profile, and account references | Chats and profile |

*In short: choose a valid username for your account and use your display name to show your personal style.*

Example:

> **Username:** shadow\.play
>
> **Display name:** 𝓢𝓱𝓪𝓭𝓸𝔀 ♡
>
> **Stylish display name:** ꧁༺ Shadow ༻꧂

**Remember:** Usernames should be unique, while display names are not obliged to be unique. Thus, you can choose an interesting display name although it has been used by another Discord member.

## How to Change Your Discord Username and Display Name


![Discord Profile Names Explained](https://aestheticfonts.in/images/discord-profile-names-explained.webp)


You can change both names from your Discord account settings. The steps are slightly different for usernames and display names.

### How to Change Your Discord Username

1. Open Discord on your computer or phone.
2. Click your profile or User Settings icon.
3. Select **My Account**.
4. Find the Username section and click Edit.
5. Enter your preferred username.
6. Confirm your changes.

Your new username must [follow Discord's character rules](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/213480948-How-do-I-change-my-Username) and must be available.

### How to Change Your Discord Display Name

1. Open User Settings.
2. Go to Profiles.
3. Find the Display Name field.
4. Remove your old name.
5. Paste your new stylish name.
6. Save your changes.

You can also set a different server nickname in servers where you have permission to change it.

## How to Check if a Discord Username Is Available

Discord usernames are unique, so two people cannot use exactly the same username. If someone already has your preferred name, you will need to choose another one.

Try adding a small change to your original idea. For example, if `shadow` is taken, you could try:

- `shadow.x`
- `shadow_7`
- `its.shadow`
- `shadowplay`

You can use a Discord username checker or username search tool to explore name availability, but third-party tools may not always show accurate results.

**The final check is inside Discord** when you try to save your new username.

## Tips for Choosing a Good Discord Name

A stylish name looks nice, but it should also be easy for other people to read.

Keep these points in mind when choosing your name:

- **Keep it short:** Short names are easier to remember and share.
- **Choose your style:** Pick a name that matches your gaming or personal profile.
- **Use symbols carefully:** Too many decorative characters can make a name difficult to read.
- **Check availability:** Your username must be unique.
- **Try different fonts:** Compare [bold](https://aestheticfonts.in/blog/how-to-bold-text/), cursive, gothic, and small-caps styles.
- **Check mobile appearance:** Some special characters may look different on older phones or devices.
- **Give it a personal touch:** A nickname, favourite character, or memory can make your name more meaningful.
