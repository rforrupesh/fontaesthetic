Stylish fonts are a simple way to make your social media bio, captions and comments stand out from the crowd.

## Where can you use them?

- Instagram bio and captions
- Discord nicknames and messages
- WhatsApp status
- Twitter/X posts

## How it works

Under the hood, these fonts are actually special Unicode characters that look like styled letters. Since they are Unicode, they work almost everywhere text is supported — no font installation needed.

Just type your text into the generator above, pick a style you like, and hit copy.
