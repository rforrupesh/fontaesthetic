## How to Pick the Perfect Snapchat Name

Before settling on a name, say it out loud and see how it sounds. Does it feel natural? Is it something your friends would easily remember? A good username doesn't need extra words or complicated spelling to look interesting.

A few things worth checking before you choose:

- **Easy to remember:** Pick a name that friends can recall without asking you twice.
- **Short and simple:** Avoid long spellings or too many random numbers.
- **Your own style:** Use words that match your hobbies, interests, or personality.
- **Mix and match:** Try adding words like Moon, Star, Vibes, Dream, or King to your nickname.
- **Keep private details out:** Don't use your phone number, home address, or other personal information.
- **Check if it's available:** Your favourite name might already belong to another Snapchat user. You can check Snapchat's [username requirements](https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-us/sections/5685857190804-Name-and-Username) before choosing one.

> If the name you like is already taken, don't give up on it straight away. Try changing the second word, using your initials, or adding a small number that means something to you. A little change can make the name feel more personal without making it difficult to type.

## How to Change Your Snapchat Username

Got an old username that you don't like anymore? You don't need to create a new account just to get a different one. Snapchat lets you update your username from the app settings.

Follow these steps:

1. Open Snapchat on your phone.
2. Tap your profile or Bitmoji icon.
3. Tap the gear icon to open Settings.
4. Go to **My Account** and select **Username**.
5. Tap **Change Username**.
6. Type the new username you want to use.
7. Review your choice and tap Next to confirm.

> One thing to remember: Snapchat allows a username change only once every 12 months. Your Display Name works differently and can be edited separately. So, if you just want a new name to appear on your profile and in chats, changing your Display Name might be all you need.
