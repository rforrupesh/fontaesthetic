WhatsApp uses a simple default font, but you can change how your text looks. You can use built-in formatting for bold, italic, strikethrough and monospace, or copy stylish Unicode text from a fancy font generator.

These styles work in chats, groups, profile names, About and Status, on Android, iPhone, WhatsApp Web and Desktop.

## How to Change Font Style in WhatsApp Messages

Add a symbol before and after your words, or at the start of a line.

| Text style | How to type | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Bold | `*text*` | **Hello** |
| Italic | `_text_` | *Hello* |
| Strikethrough | `~text~` | ~~Hello~~ |
| Monospace | Three backticks on both sides | `Hello` |
| Inline code | One backtick on both sides | `hello` |
| Block quote | `> text` | Quoted text |

Do not leave a space between the symbol and the text. `*text*` works, `* text *` does not.

To learn more about bold formatting, see our guide on [how to bold text](https://aestheticfonts.in/blog/how-to-bold-text/). You can also check the official [WhatsApp message formatting guide](https://faq.whatsapp.com/539178204879377).

## WhatsApp Text Tricks with Examples

| You type | You get | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `*Meeting at 5 PM*` | **Meeting at 5 PM** | Important info |
| `_Please be on time_` | *Please be on time* | Soft emphasis |
| `~Old price 999~ *Now 499*` | ~~Old price 999~~ **Now 499** | Offers |
| `*_Very important_*` | ***Very important*** | Bold + italic |
| `*~Cancelled~*` | **~~Cancelled~~** | Bold + strikethrough |

You can also style only part of a sentence: `Tomorrow is *Rahul's birthday*, bring a _gift_.`

**Without symbols:**
- **Android:** Long-press the text, tap the three dots and choose a style.
- **iPhone:** Select the text, tap the arrow and choose BIU.
- **WhatsApp Web / Desktop:** Select the text and press `Ctrl + B` or `Ctrl + I` (`Cmd` on Mac).

## How to Make Lists and Quotes in WhatsApp

**Bulleted list:** Start a line with `*` or `-` and a space.

* First point
* Second point
* Third point

**Numbered list:** Type a number, a period and a space.

1. Open WhatsApp.
2. Select a chat.
3. Write your message.

**Block quote:** Type `>` and a space before your sentence.

> This is an important message.

## How to Change WhatsApp Profile Name and About Font

Fancy Unicode text works in your profile name, About and group names. WhatsApp's own bold and italic symbols do not work in the profile name, so use a generator.

For example, Rahul can look like this:

- 𝓡𝓪𝓱𝓾𝓵
- 𝐑𝐚𝐡𝐮𝐥
- 𝕽𝖆𝖍𝖚𝖑
- ℝ𝕒𝕙𝕦𝕝

To change your profile name:

1. Open WhatsApp and go to Settings.
2. Tap your profile name.
3. Replace it with the copied fancy text.
4. Save your changes.

To change About, open your profile, tap About and paste your text.

## Can You Change the Default WhatsApp Font?

No. WhatsApp has no setting for this and follows your phone's system font. Some Android phones (Samsung, Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo) let you change the system font in Settings, and WhatsApp will follow it. iPhone does not allow a custom system font.

## How to Change WhatsApp Font Size

Font size and font style are different settings.

**On Android:** Open WhatsApp Settings, tap Chats, then Font size, and choose Small, Medium or Large.

**On iPhone:** Open Settings, go to Display & Brightness, tap Text Size and move the slider.

## Why Do Some Fancy Fonts Not Work in WhatsApp?

Fancy text uses Unicode characters, and some older devices cannot show every one. It may appear as a box or question mark. If that happens, try another style. Characters can also look slightly different on Android and iPhone.

Also remember that fancy text is not found by normal search, and screen readers may skip it. For long or important messages, use simple text.

## Conclusion

WhatsApp lets you format text with bold, italic, strikethrough, monospace, lists and quotes. For decorative lettering, use a fancy font generator and paste the Unicode text into your chats, profile name, About or Status. You do not need any extra font app.
