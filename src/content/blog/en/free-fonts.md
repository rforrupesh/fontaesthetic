Yes. These free fonts are open source and can be used commercially.

### Can I find paid or premium fonts here?
No. This library only has open-source fonts that are free for everyone. Premium fonts need a paid license from their creators, so we do not share them.

### Do I need to give credit?

### Which are the best free fonts for logos?
Montserrat, Poppins, Playfair Display, Bebas Neue, and Cinzel are popular choices.

### Are these free fonts good for Hindi?
Hind, Noto Sans, and Baloo 2 support Devanagari, so they work well for Hindi text.
