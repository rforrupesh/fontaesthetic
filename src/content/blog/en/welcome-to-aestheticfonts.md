## What Are Fancy Fonts and How Do They Work?

Fancy fonts are stylish-looking letters created using special Unicode characters. Unlike traditional fonts, which change how regular letters appear on your screen, Unicode text uses different character codes to produce decorative-looking letters.

For example, the word Hello can look like this:

- Normal: Hello
- Bold: 𝐇𝐞𝐥𝐥𝐨
- Cursive: 𝓗𝓮𝓵𝓵𝓸
- Gothic: ℌ𝔢𝔩𝔩𝔬
- Double Struck: ℍ𝕖𝕝𝕝𝕠
- Bubble: Ⓗⓔⓛⓛⓞ

These characters can be copied just like ordinary text. That's why you can paste them into many social media platforms without installing a separate font file.

However, not every device supports every Unicode character. Some letters may appear as empty boxes or look slightly different depending on the platform.
