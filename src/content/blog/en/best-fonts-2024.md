## Overview

Every year a few font styles become especially popular on social media. Here's a roundup of what's trending in 2024.

## 1. Bold Serif

Clean and confident — great for headings and bios that need to stand out immediately.

## 2. Bubble Text

Playful and fun, popular among younger audiences on Instagram and TikTok bios.

## 3. Cursive / Script

Elegant and personal, often used for quotes or aesthetic feed captions.

## 4. Strikethrough Combos

Used creatively to show "before and after" style jokes or emphasis.

## How to Try Them

Use the generator on the homepage, type any text, and scroll through the live preview to find a style that matches your aesthetic.

## Final Thoughts

Trends change, but a good stylish font never really goes out of fashion for personal branding online.
