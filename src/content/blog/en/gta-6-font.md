*[Grand Theft Auto VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI)* (GTA 6) is out now, released on **November 19, 2026**. Ever since the first trailer, one question keeps coming up: what font is that logo? Short answer: it's Pricedown, with a hand-drawn "VI" on top. Below is the long answer, including who made the font, how to get the look yourself, and which fonts come closest when you can't use the original.

## What Font Is Used in the GTA 6 Logo?

**Pricedown** is the typeface behind the main wordmark. It was designed by Canadian type designer **[Ray Larabie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Larabie)** and published by **Typodermic Fonts** in 2000. Larabie has said the style was inspired by early-1970s display lettering, the kind you'd see on game-show titles and old TV graphics. That chunky, slightly goofy, very confident look is exactly why it suits a series built on satire.

Most people first noticed Pricedown on the box art of *Grand Theft Auto III*, and it has been tied to the franchise ever since. In the GTA VI logo, it sets the stacked "Grand Theft Auto" text: heavy strokes, tight spacing, and squared-off curves.

The "VI" is a different story. It is custom artwork, not a character you can type. That's why typing "VI" in Pricedown never looks like the real logo. If you want the full effect, you'll need to draw or trace your own numeral.

## GTA 6 Logo Typography and Design

Look closely at the logo and a few things stand out:

- **Bold lettering:** The strokes are very thick, so the text stays readable even at thumbnail size.
- **Condensed proportions:** Letters sit close together and look compact, which lets the wordmark stack neatly.
- **Custom Roman numeral:** The "VI" has its own shape and weight, separate from the font.
- **Decorative details:** Gradients, outlines, and shadows in the artwork give it that glossy, sun-soaked finish.

Pricedown does not include any of these effects by default. The look comes from how the text is styled afterwards, which is something you can copy in any design tool.

## GTA 6 Logo Colors

The GTA VI artwork leans on a neon, sunset-style palette that reminds people of Vice City: hot pink, bright green, deep black, and white, with orange and purple tones in some posters. Rockstar uses different color combinations on different pieces, so there isn't one single official swatch.

If you want a close match for your own designs, these are good starting points (approximate, picked by eye, not official values):

- Hot pink: `#FF3EA5`
- Neon green: `#2BFF88`
- Sunset orange: `#FF8A3D`
- Deep black: `#0B0B0F`
- Off white: `#F5F5F5`

For an exact match, open the official artwork in an image editor and use the eyedropper tool on the part you like.

## Fonts Similar to GTA 6

Pricedown is the one to try first. If you can't use it, or want a different flavor, these free options get you most of the way there:

- **Bangers:** comic-style and punchy. Good for energetic thumbnails.
- **Bebas Neue:** clean and condensed. Looks modern and tidy.
- **Anton:** very tall and heavy. Great for headlines.
- **Lilita One:** rounded and friendly. Closest to Pricedown's playful feel.
- **Titan One:** super thick with soft corners.

All of them are available on Google Fonts. Other display [bold fonts](https://aestheticfonts.in/bold/) can work too, especially ones with condensed capitals and heavy strokes. When comparing, look at letter width, corner shape, and stroke thickness. None will match the original exactly, but a bit of outline and color goes a long way.

## How to Create GTA 6 Style Text

You can do this in Canva, Photoshop, Photopea, or any editor that supports text effects.

1. [Install Pricedown](https://typodermicfonts.com/pricedown/) or select a similar display font.
2. Type your text in capitals. Pricedown looks best in all caps.
3. Increase the size, then tighten the letter spacing slightly so the letters nearly touch.
4. Add a gradient fill from pink to orange, or pink to purple, and put a thick dark outline behind the text.
5. Add a soft shadow or glow in a contrasting color, and place the text on a dark or sunset background. For the Roman numeral, draw your own shape with the pen tool or use a bold numeral from a similar font.
6. Export the finished design as a PNG with a transparent background, or as a JPG if you don't need transparency.

A Unicode font generator can create alternative text styles for usernames and social media profiles, but it does not reproduce the actual Pricedown typeface.

## GTA 6 Font vs GTA 6 Logo

A font is a set of letters you can type with. A logo is a finished graphic. The GTA VI logo uses Pricedown lettering, but also a custom Roman numeral, specific spacing, color treatment, and effects. Installing the font gives you the letters, not the logo.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the GTA 6 logo font called?

The main wordmark uses Pricedown. The "VI" is custom artwork made by Rockstar.

### Who designed Pricedown?

Ray Larabie designed Pricedown, and Typodermic Fonts publishes it.

### When was Pricedown released?

Pricedown was released in 2000 and was inspired by early-1970s display lettering.

### Is the entire GTA 6 logo available as a font?

No. The full logo includes custom artwork that isn't part of Pricedown.

### Is Pricedown free to download?

Typodermic offers it for free for personal use, but commercial use needs a paid licence. Always read the licence on the official page before using it in a project that earns money.

### Is Pricedown available in Canva?

Canva doesn't always carry Pricedown, but you can upload your own font file on a Pro plan. If you're on the free plan, use Lilita One or Bangers instead.

### Can I use Pricedown for YouTube thumbnails?

You can use the font in your own designs, subject to its licence. Official Rockstar Games logos and artwork may have separate copyright and trademark restrictions.

## Conclusion

Pricedown is the typeface used for the main GTA VI wordmark, and the custom "VI" is what makes the logo special. If you want the look, start with Pricedown, add a pink-to-orange gradient and a thick outline, and draw your own numeral. If you can't use Pricedown, Lilita One or Bangers are the nearest free picks.
