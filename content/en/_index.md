## What is a fancy text generator?

A fancy text generator converts normal letters into stylized Unicode characters — bold, italic, script, gothic, circled, and dozens more. Each style is a real, readable character, not an image, so it copies and pastes cleanly into Instagram, TikTok, Discord, WhatsApp, and anywhere else that accepts text.
