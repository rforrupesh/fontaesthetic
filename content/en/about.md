FancyText helps you turn plain text into 60+ stylized Unicode fonts — bold, italic, script, gothic, bubble, and more — for use anywhere plain text works: Instagram, TikTok, Discord, WhatsApp, and beyond.

Every style is real Unicode text, not an image, so it copies and pastes cleanly without losing formatting.

The tool is free, requires no sign-up, and works entirely in your browser.
